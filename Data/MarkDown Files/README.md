# Guideline sources

The app searches only the files registered in `artemis/guidelines/sources.py`: Ugandan guidance
only (owner, 2026-11-25). Both files are text extracts of one document:

| File | Document | Edition | Printed pages | Where to get it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `HIV_Guidelines_ART.extraction.md` | Consolidated Guidelines for the Prevention and Treatment of HIV and AIDS in Uganda (antiretroviral therapy chapters) | Ministry of Health, Republic of Uganda, November 2022 | 234 to 284 | see below |
| `HIV_Guidelines_Co_infectionScreening.extraction.md` | The same guidelines (co-infections and screening chapters) | November 2022 | 152 to 202 | see below |

Citations name the printed page of the book ("Uganda HIV guidelines 2022, p.255"). In the PDF the
printed page is the PDF page minus 30 (the front matter), checked on three pages.

Where to get the PDF: a copy is hosted by the UNAIDS HIV Prevention Coalition,
<https://hivpreventioncoalition.unaids.org/sites/default/files/attachments/consolidated_guidelines_for_the_prevention_and_treatment_of_hiv_and_aids_in_uganda_2022.pdf>.
It is not the Ministry of Health's own site; an official Ministry link has not been confirmed.

The source PDFs, and the guidelines from outside Uganda, are kept on the owner's computer only
(not in the repository) since 2026-11-26 (plan item 2B.6). `Example Output/` holds old sample
outputs of the app.
